EverRoad bundles two open-licensed typefaces. The font files themselves are
processed by Vite and land in dist/assets/ with hashed names; the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 texts beside this note are copied verbatim so that the licence
travels with the binaries into every packaged build, as the OFL requires.

  Quicksand       — Quicksand-OFL.txt
  JetBrains Mono  — JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt

Do not delete these. See src/fonts/README.md in the source tree for why the
fonts are self-hosted and how the subset was chosen.
